# Support & Issue Reporting

If you encounter issues, this guide will help you gather the right information.

---

## 🐞 Reporting a Bug

Please include:

1. **Description of the issue**  
2. **Steps to reproduce**  
3. **Expected behavior**  
4. **Actual behavior**  
5. **Klipper log** (`klippy.log`)  
6. **Your macro configuration**  

---

## 📄 Useful Commands

To gather diagnostic information:
STATUS GET_POSITION M105 M122

---

## 🧪 Common Debugging Steps

- Ensure macros are loaded (`RESTART`)  
- Check for undefined variables  
- Verify slicer is passing correct parameters  
- Confirm Qidi LCD is calling the custom macros
