# Filament Management

The suite includes two staged routines:

- FILAMENT_LOAD  
- FILAMENT_UNLOAD  

---

## FILAMENT_LOAD

### Behavior:

1. Fast feed (70%)  
2. Slow feed (30%)  

This prevents:
- grinding  
- overshooting  
- inconsistent priming  

---

## FILAMENT_UNLOAD

### Behavior:

1. Slow pull (30%)  
2. Fast retract (70%)  

This prevents:
- heat‑break jams  
- filament snapping
